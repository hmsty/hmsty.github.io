      method

      pipeline

      • Load. JSON export into SQLite: one row per conversation, message and tool call.
      • Label. Written rules for topics (by title) and habits (by phrasing), each with a plain definition.
      • Check. Read 20 flagged messages per rule for precision, and 120 random messages for what the rules miss.
      • Cut. Rules under 75% precision were dropped. Headline rates come from the hand labels.
      • Protect. Any conversation with a work or personal signal goes to withheld. Two audits of public topics: 3 to 5 leaks in 58, then 1 in 100 after a stricter rule.
      • Publish aggregates only. Raw data and labels never leave my machine.
